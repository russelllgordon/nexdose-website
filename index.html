Everything you need for your shots. Nothing you don't.

Nexdose was built by someone who injects on a schedule and got tired of trackers that were too complicated and asked for too much.

Log in one tap

Tap a due shot to log it. Or log from a Lock Screen widget, your Apple Watch, or Siri.

Smart site rotation

A body map shows where you've injected and suggests the next spot, so you don't keep using the same one.

Flexible schedules

Weekly, daily, monthly, every weekday or custom. Several doses a day, on and off cycles, and mid-cycle starts.

Reminders

Optional notifications at each dose time. Move or skip a single dose without changing your schedule.

Dose increases

Plan your titration once, and Nexdose moves you up to the next dose on schedule. Plus

Weight and body stats

Track weight, body fat and muscle against a goal, with charts from one month to all time.

Levels

See a half-life graph of your estimated levels between shots. Plus

Supply tracking

Know how much is left in each pen or vial before you run out. Plus

Reports for your provider

Export your history as a clean PDF report Plus or a CSV file.

Private by design

No sign-up. No ads. No personal questions. No analytics or tracking in the app. Everything you enter is stored on your iPhone, and optionally in your own private iCloud. We never see your data.
