How to use Nexdose

Short guides for setting up your shots and getting the most from Nexdose. Steps are for version 1.1.

Add your first shot

  1. Tap the + button.
  2. Enter the peptide or medicine name and your dose.
  3. Choose a schedule (see below) and save.

To change it later, open the All Peptides page, where you can add, edit and remove everything in one place.

Set a schedule

Choose Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Every weekday or Custom. The day of the week comes from the start date you pick, so for a weekly GLP-1 shot, set the start date to the day you inject.

  • Already mid-cycle? Pick the day you took the first dose of this cycle as the start date, and Nexdose works out where you are.
  • More than one dose a day? Set Doses per day to 2 or 3 and choose a time for each.

Log a shot

On the home screen, tap a shot that's due. Want a different site? Tap the body picture on its row first. Missed logging one? Tap a past day on the calendar and log it there.

You can also log without opening the app:

  • Widgets: add the Nexdose widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen, then tap the check.
  • Apple Watch: open Nexdose on your watch and tap the check. The watch-face complication shows your next shot.
  • Siri: ask Siri to log your shot in Nexdose.

Logged by mistake? Tap Undo in the message that appears.

Move or skip a dose

Swipe right on a due shot and choose Move to pick a new date and time for just that dose, or skip it if you missed it. You can also press and hold a due shot for Edit, Move or skip, and Remove. Your regular schedule carries on unchanged.

Rotate injection sites

Nexdose suggests your next site on a body map and shows which areas you've used recently. Free includes a simple map with 12 sites, and Plus adds a detailed map with 72. Prefer reminders and logging only? Turn off site rotation in Settings.

Turn on reminders

Turn on Remind me at each dose time in a peptide's schedule. If reminders don't appear, check iPhone Settings > Notifications > Nexdose.

Track weight and body stats

Log your weight, body fat and muscle, set a goal, and see your progress over 1M, 3M, 6M, 1Y or All. With Plus, import past weights from a CSV file or Apple Health.

Export a report

Open the menu and choose Export. Create a CSV file (free) or a PDF report (Plus) that shows each peptide's current dose and schedule. If you turn on notes and symptoms in Settings, they're included too. Share it with your provider or save it to Files.

Sync and lock

  • iCloud sync: if you're signed in to iCloud with iCloud turned on for Nexdose, your data syncs through your own private iCloud account.
  • Face ID lock: lock Nexdose with Face ID or your passcode in Settings.
  • Background: pick a color, make your own, or use one of your photos.

Plus features Plus

Auto dose-up (titration)

Plan your dose increases once, for example a few weeks at each step, and Nexdose moves you up to the next dose on schedule.

Supply tracking

Enter what's in each pen or vial, and Nexdose counts down as you log so you know when to reorder.

Levels

A half-life graph shows your estimated levels between shots. It is an estimate for your own reference, not medical advice.

Cycles

Set on and off cycles, such as days on and days off or weeks on and weeks off, once, and Nexdose follows them.
