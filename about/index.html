Why I built Nexdose

Placeholder: Russ's original About page text goes here, copied from the Google Site so it's in his own words.

I'm a peptide user, and I built Nexdose for people like me, and for friends and family with the same frustrations: rotating injection sites, remembering doses and schedules, and planning cycles.

Why I deleted other apps before I even got started

  • Personal questions. I wanted a tracker, not a questionnaire about my goals, health and weight.
  • Create an account. A log on my phone shouldn't need a sign-up.
  • Too complex. Cluttered graphs, tables and logs. Nexdose uses a simple calendar.
  • All the best stuff costs. Nexdose's free version covers the basics, and Plus is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
