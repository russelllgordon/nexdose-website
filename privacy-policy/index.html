Nexdose Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Nexdose ("the app") is developed by Russell Gordon in St. George, Utah. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Nexdose.

The short version

We do not collect your data. Nexdose has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party tracking, and no servers of ours that receive your information.

Information you enter

You may enter peptide names, doses, injection sites, dates and times, schedules, notes, and optionally body weight and a goal weight. This information is stored on your device so the app can work. We do not receive it, and we cannot see it.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is enabled for Nexdose, your app data is stored in your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit so it can sync between your devices. This data is protected by your Apple ID and is handled under Apple's terms and privacy policy. It is not accessible to us. You can turn this off in iPhone Settings and remove the data from iCloud storage at any time.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Nexdose schedules local notifications on your device. These are created and delivered by your iPhone. No notification data is sent to us.

Exports and sharing

When you export a PDF or CSV, the file is created on your device. It goes only where you choose to send it, such as Mail, Messages or Files. We do not receive exports.

Feedback

If you choose to email us from within the app or at contact@nexdose.app, we receive only what you write and the email address you send from. We use it only to reply to you.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address (unless you write to us), location, contacts, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage analytics or crash data. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Health information

Nexdose lets you record personal health-related entries such as doses and weight. This information stays on your device (and your private iCloud if enabled). It is not shared with us or anyone else. Nexdose does not provide medical advice.

Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete shots and peptides in the app, or delete the app to remove local data. To remove iCloud copies, go to iPhone Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Nexdose.

Children

Nexdose is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. Material changes will be reflected in the app's next update.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email contact@nexdose.app.
