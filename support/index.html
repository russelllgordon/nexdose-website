Need help or want to share an idea? Email contact@nexdose.app and we'll reply as soon as we can. Including your iPhone model and iOS version helps.

Frequently asked questions

How do I log a shot?

On the home screen, tap a shot that is due. To change the injection site first, tap the body picture on its row. See the how-to guide for widgets, Apple Watch and Siri.

How do I add or edit a peptide?

Tap the + button to add one. Open the All Peptides page to edit a dose, schedule or reminders, or to remove one. Your history is kept.

I missed a dose or want to take it later. What do I do?

Swipe right on the due shot and choose Move to pick a new date and time for just that dose, or skip it. Your regular schedule continues.

I started my cycle already. Can I set that up?

Yes. In the schedule section, pick the day you took your first dose of this cycle as the start date. Nexdose works out where you are.

Can I take more than one dose a day?

Yes. Set Doses per day to 2 or 3 and choose a time for each.

How do reminders work?

Turn on "Remind me at each dose time" in a peptide's schedule. If notifications are off, enable them in iPhone Settings > Notifications > Nexdose.

How do I export my data?

Open the menu and choose Export. You can create a CSV file, or a PDF report with Plus, and share it with your provider or save it to Files.

Does Nexdose sync between my devices?

Yes, if you are signed in to iCloud and have iCloud turned on for Nexdose. Sync uses your private iCloud account. We cannot see it.

How do I delete my data?

Delete shots or peptides inside the app. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. To also remove iCloud copies, go to iPhone Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Nexdose, then delete the data.

Is the calculator medical advice?

No. The calculators only do arithmetic. Always confirm any dose or reconstitution with your healthcare provider.

Is Nexdose free?

Yes. The core app is free, with no ads and no account. Nexdose Plus is an optional one-time $14.99 purchase.

Medical disclaimer
