How to use them together

  1. Before each shot, look at the rotation chart for the site with the oldest date, or one you haven't used yet.
  2. Write today's date in that site's next empty box.
  3. Add a row to the shot log with the date, dose and that site's number. Add your weight if you're tracking it.

Works for a weekly GLP-1 shot such as semaglutide or tirzepatide, and for daily or other injections too.
