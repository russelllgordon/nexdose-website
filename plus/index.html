Questions about Plus

Is Plus a subscription?

No. Plus is a single $14.99 purchase. You pay once and keep it.

Can my partner or family use it?

Yes. Plus works with Apple Family Sharing, so everyone in your family group gets it.

I installed Nexdose 1.0. Do I need to buy Plus?

No. If you first installed version 1.0, Plus is yours free as a thank-you.

What does the Tip Jar do?

Nothing extra. Tips of $1.99, $4.99 or $9.99 are an optional way to say thanks and don't unlock anything.

Does Plus change my privacy?

No. Purchases go through Apple. Nexdose still has no account, no ads and no tracking.
